Our Story

Baking is more than a business.

Velvet Crumb started in a home kitchen, with one oven and a habit of saying yes to every strange, wonderful cake request that came our way. What began as birthday cakes for friends turned into a full studio — but the instinct never changed: listen first, then bake something that actually feels like the person it's for.

We still taste-test everything ourselves, still hand-pipe every border, and still get a little nervous before a big wedding delivery. That's the part we never want to lose.

"Every cake has a story. We simply help you tell it."
